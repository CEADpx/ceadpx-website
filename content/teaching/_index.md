The CEAD Lab develops educational resources that connect continuum models,
numerical methods, scientific software, and verification. 

## Featured open course and book

### ME/BME 736 — Advanced Finite Element Methods

This Fall 2026 graduate course develops finite element methods as both a
mathematical and computational framework. Heat conduction and solid mechanics
serve as recurring model problems, and FEniCSx is used for implementation and
numerical investigation. The accompanying open book is being developed alongside
the course and will continue to grow as new chapters and computational examples
are completed.

[Read the open finite element methods book](https://ceadpx.github.io/finite-element-methods/)

## Teaching connected to the lab

Current and recent courses taught by Prof. Prashant K. Jha include:

- **ME 736 — Advanced Finite Element Methods:** Fall 2026
- **ME 322 — Machine Design I:** Fall 2024–Fall 2026
- **ME 316 — Solid Mechanics:** Spring 2026
- **ME 428/528 — Applied Finite Element Analysis:** Fall 2025

Together, these courses span mechanics, engineering design, numerical
approximation, and reproducible scientific computing—the same foundations that
support the lab's research in computational engineering analysis and design.
